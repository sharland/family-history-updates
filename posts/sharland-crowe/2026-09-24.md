*Family history — 24 September*

An evening with the birth, marriage and death indexes, and most of it is about Margaret Mary Sharland's brothers and sisters. It also answers a question I last asked Gran Sharland about in the mid-2000s and which I brought up with Rosemary.

*The mystery signature on Clive and Joan's 1940 wedding menu is solved as Clive's aunt.* Nobody, Joan included, knew who "H. M. Sharland" was. She was Helen M. Sharland, born Barwick, who married Margaret Mary's eldest brother John Howard Sharland in Bournemouth in 1911. Their daughter Barbara, born in 1914, is the cousin who married Harold Windebank in 1939.

*Helen was not the only aunt at the wedding.* The signature beside hers, initially read as "Keith Mitchell", is almost certainly "Kit Mitchell": Margaret Mary's sister Katherine Maud, who married William Mitchell in 1915. Their teenage daughter signed with them.

*A brother nobody knew about.* Hannah and William Claridge Sharland had a sixth child the tree had lost: William, born in Bournemouth in 1895, seven months before his father died. He lived there until 1951.

*Robert Montague Sharland*, another brother, was a corporal in the Royal Horse Artillery and was killed in France in May 1917. He had married Elizabeth Wright in Coventry only eighteen months earlier. I was glad to find she remarried in 1924. I tried to find children registered to her and Robert M but nothing came up.

*Family history — 3rd October*

When we were talking about the menu card it was mentioned that Peggy had gone to NZ to visit the Sharland branch there. I think I've found how the New Zealand branch split off. Your great grandfather William Claridge Sharland had a younger brother, Henry James, born in Hackney in 1851, who vanishes from English records after 1871. A Henry James Sharland married Amelia Jane Coxhead in New Zealand in 1889 and died there in 1921 aged 70, exactly the right age. His grandson was Arthur Sharland of Whangārei, who died in 2016. If I am right, Arthur and Clive were second cousins, which would explain Peggy's visit to the New Zealand branch.

Although it might have been recorded before I've found Dick Crowe's  case file for his gallantry award at the library. The case file covers his role as a part-time fire officer, formerly head of Weston's Auxiliary Fire Service, and how he "continually toured the fire ground" through both nights of raids. His superiors praised "his disregard for danger, leadership and qualities as an Officer". The committee noted "some gallantry element" and put him on its half-yearly list. I am still looking for the published result.

*Edgar Eaton Sharland's grandfather was an exciseman.* The birth certificate of Edgar's father, Edward Solomon (Battersea, 11 April 1849), gives Christopher Robins Solomon as a Supervisor of Excise, which explains why his children were born everywhere from Ireland to Bristol, Swindon, London and Barnstaple.

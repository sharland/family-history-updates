*Family history — 10 October*

*Sybil Crowe's mother has a maiden name at last.* This is Granny Joan's Gran.  The tree only called her Mary Ellen. Thomas Henry Rixom's marriage was listed under "Rixon": he married Mary Ellen Sturman in Northamptonshire in 1891. She was born at Lyme Regis in 1864, very probably the daughter of William Sturman and Ellen Grinter, who married nearby in 1860. I think she died at Rugby in 1950, aged 85. Sybil's line now runs back to a Dorset family I certainly hadn't come across before. @Rosemary This means that your Great Grandparents and Great Great Grandparents surnames are now all known.

*I purchased a digital copy of Edgar Eaton Sharland's will.* In 1956 he left "all my dental equipment" to Clive, by then in Bulawayo, and gives Robert Edgar's address as the United Africa Company's accounts department in Kano, Northern Nigeria which I know won't be new to you @Peter. The rest was for Margaret Mary for life, then half to Peggy and a quarter to each brother.

*Christopher Robins Solomon, the exciseman, married twice.* He married Elizabeth Shaw at St Ives in 1835; an Elizabeth "Lebby" Solomon who died at St Austell in 1841 is very probably her. In 1844 he married Mary Rogers Perry at Marlborough, a widow, born Mary Rogers Turner. Edgar's line comes from this second marriage.

*Edgar Eaton Sharland's father, Edward Solomon, was born in Battersea on 11 April 1849, not in 1840 as Clive remembered.* His birth certificate shows his father, Christopher Robins Solomon, was a Supervisor of Excise, which explains why the family's children were born everywhere from Ireland to Bristol, Swindon, London and Barnstaple: excise officers were moved from post to post every few years.

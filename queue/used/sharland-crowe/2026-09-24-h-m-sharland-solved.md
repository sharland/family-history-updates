*The mystery guest at Clive and Joan's 1940 wedding is solved.* The menu card was signed "H. M. Sharland", and nobody, Joan included, knew who that was. She was Helen M. Sharland, born Barwick, who married Margaret Mary's eldest brother John Howard Sharland in Bournemouth in 1911. Their daughter Barbara, born in 1914, is the cousin whose wedding to Harold Windebank the Family Story describes in 1939. Helen was Clive's aunt, seated among his cousins.

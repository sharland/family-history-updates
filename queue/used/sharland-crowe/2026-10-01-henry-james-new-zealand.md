*I think I have found how the New Zealand Sharlands connect to us.* William Claridge Sharland had a younger brother, Henry James, born in Hackney in 1851, who disappears from English records after 1871. A Henry James Sharland married Amelia Jane Coxhead in New Zealand in 1889 and died there in 1921 aged 70, which is exactly the right age. His grandson was Arthur Sharland of Whangārei, who died in 2016. If this is right, Arthur and Clive were second cousins, and it would explain Peggy's visit to the New Zealand branch. One more document, his 1921 death record, should settle it.

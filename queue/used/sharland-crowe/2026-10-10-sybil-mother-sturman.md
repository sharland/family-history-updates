*Sybil Crowe's mother has a maiden name at last.* The tree only ever called her Mary Ellen. Thomas Henry Rixom's marriage had been hiding in the index under "Rixon": he married Mary Ellen Sturman in Northamptonshire in the summer of 1891. She was born at Lyme Regis in 1864, very probably the daughter of William Sturman and Ellen Grinter, who married there in 1860, and I think she is the Mary E. Rixom who died at Rugby in 1950, aged 85. So Sybil's line now runs back to a Dorset family none of us had heard of.

*Margaret Mary Sharland's brother Robert Montague Sharland died in France on 20 May 1917.* The war graves record shows he was a corporal in the "Warwick" Battery of the Royal Horse Artillery, aged 28, and is buried at Monchy-le-Preux. It also shows something the tree did not know: he was married, to Elizabeth Ellen, who was living in Coventry.

*Gran's aunt Bunny married her second cousin Ted Crowe in 1924, and the family called it "The Bird Wedding".* Bride and groom were both Crowes, and the certificate shows the ceremony at Holy Trinity, Weston-super-Mare, on 24 September was performed by Ted's father, the Rector of Cavan. The 1977 family history adds that the trousseau came from a shop called "Sparrows". I think Dick Crowe, Gran's father, signed as a witness, beside Dolly.

*Edgar Eaton Sharland's will has arrived, and it is a small family portrait from 1956.* He left "all my dental equipment" to Clive, by then in Bulawayo, and gives Robert Edgar's address as the United Africa Company's accounts department in Kano, Northern Nigeria, which I had not known. Everything else was for Margaret Mary for her life, and then Peggy, who lived with them at Barton-on-Sea, was to have a half and her brothers a quarter each.

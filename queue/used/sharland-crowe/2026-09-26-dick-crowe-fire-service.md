*Dick Crowe was recommended for a gallantry award for the Weston-super-Mare blitz of June 1942.* I found his case file at the library. As a part-time fire officer, formerly head of Weston's Auxiliary Fire Service, he "continually toured the fire ground" through the two nights of raids on 27 to 29 June, and his officers wrote that "his disregard for danger, leadership and qualities as an Officer contributed very largely to the success of the operations". The committee noted "some gallantry element" and put him on the half-yearly list; I am now looking for the London Gazette entry.

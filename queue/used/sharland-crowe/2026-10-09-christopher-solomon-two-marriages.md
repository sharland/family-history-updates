*Christopher Robins Solomon, the exciseman, married twice.* The tree has him marrying Elizabeth Shaw at St Ives in 1835, and an Elizabeth "Lebby" Solomon died at St Austell in 1841, very probably her. In 1844 he married Mary Rogers Perry at Marlborough. She was a widow herself, born Mary Rogers Turner, and had married a John Perry there in 1837. Edgar's line comes from this second marriage.

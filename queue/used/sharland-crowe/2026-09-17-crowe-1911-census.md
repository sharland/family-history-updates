*The whole Crowe household is on the 1911 census, at the Royal Pier Hotel in Weston-super-Mare.* Gran's grandfather John Crowe, 65, is down as a consulting engineer and her grandmother Laura, 48, as the hotel keeper. All four children were at home: Dolly and Bunny helping in the hotel, Dick Crowe (Gran's father), 18, still at school, and their sister Marjorie, 14.

*The Mitchell cousins at Clive and Joan's wedding now have a place in the tree.* Margaret Mary's sister Katherine Maud Sharland married William Mitchell in Bournemouth in 1915, and had four children: Kenneth, Kathleen, Doreen and Derek. Doreen, aged 18, signed the 1940 menu card, and the signature beside hers, long read as "Keith Mitchell", turns out to be "Kit Mitchell": very probably Katherine Maud herself. So Clive had two aunts at his wedding, Kit and Helen, sitting together.

Sharland and Crowe family history — updates from Brian

Short updates from Brian on what he has been finding out about the family, posted first to the family WhatsApp group and kept here so they can be read again. Living relatives are referred to by first name and initial only. Replies are welcome on WhatsApp.

Family history — 24 September

Sent on Thursday 24 September 2026

An evening with the birth, marriage and death indexes, and most of it is about Margaret Mary Sharland's brothers and sisters. It also answers a question I last asked Gran Sharland about in the mid-2000s and which I brought up with Rosemary.

The mystery signature on Clive and Joan's 1940 wedding menu is solved as Clive's aunt. Nobody, Joan included, knew who "H. M. Sharland" was. She was Helen M. Sharland, born Barwick, who married Margaret Mary's eldest brother John Howard Sharland in Bournemouth in 1911. Their daughter Barbara, born in 1914, is the cousin who married Harold Windebank in 1939.

Helen was not the only aunt at the wedding. The signature beside hers, initially read as "Keith Mitchell", is almost certainly "Kit Mitchell": Margaret Mary's sister Katherine Maud, who married William Mitchell in 1915. Their teenage daughter signed with them.

A brother nobody knew about. Hannah and William Claridge Sharland had a sixth child the tree had lost: William, born in Bournemouth in 1895, seven months before his father died. He lived there until 1951.

Robert Montague Sharland, another brother, was a corporal in the Royal Horse Artillery and was killed in France in May 1917. He had married Elizabeth Wright in Coventry only eighteen months earlier. I was glad to find she remarried in 1924. I tried to find children registered to her and Robert M but nothing came up.

Family history — 21 September

Sent on Monday 21 September 2026

I have been busy since the summer and it has been a while since I wrote, so here is a catch-up on some recent document finds. Most of these confirm certain things.

Marriage certificate for Joan Sharland's aunt Bunny when married her second cousin Ted Crowe in 1924, otherwise known as "The Bird Wedding". The certificate shows the ceremony at Holy Trinity, Weston-super-Mare, was performed by Ted's father, the Rector of Cavan. An interesting find was being able to pattern match a scrawl of a signature in the witnesses to that of RJS Crowe using his signature on Joan and Clive's menu card from their wedding.

I also have the 1911 census for the Royal Pier Hotel. Joan Sharland's grandfather John Crowe, 65, is a consulting engineer and her grandmother Laura, 48, the hotel keeper. All four children were at home, including Dick, 18, still at school.

I am also starting to compile research dossiers (leaning heavily on AI to do this). If anyone wants a copy of the dossiers for any of these individuals let me know. Although the conventional standard is married name first maiden name second for the sake of grouping within my records I am using maiden name with their married name in brackets.
